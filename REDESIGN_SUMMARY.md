## Summary

This comprehensive redesign transforms the SF Street Fair Coalition website from a basic Bootstrap template into a modern, vibrant, and engaging web presence that truly reflects the energy and pride of San Francisco's street fair culture. The design incorporates Pride-inspired colors, smooth animations, improved user experience, and a professional yet approachable aesthetic that will resonate with visitors and potential coalition members.

The website now stands as a proud digital representation of the coalition's mission: **Community, Culture, and Commerce**.
